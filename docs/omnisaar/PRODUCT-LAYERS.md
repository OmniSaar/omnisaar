- Email Marketing

- People and companies
- Orders and products

- Cases and tasks

- Workflows
- Schedules

- Approvals

- Shopify
- Meta / WhatsApp
- Email providers
- Payments
- Shipping
- Additional provider connectors

## Cross-cutting platform
- Event & Activity Ledger
- Workspace / white-label configuration
- Identity resolution
- Security and permissions
- Observability and reporting

Product domains remain in this monorepo until independent release cadence, deployment/runtime isolation, or ownership makes extraction materially useful.
